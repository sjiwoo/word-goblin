🧌 Encrypt your backend URL

Everything on this page runs locally in your browser — the URL and passphrase are never sent anywhere, and nothing is saved when you close it. Paste your Apps Script web-app URL, choose a passphrase, and copy the result into js/config.js. The encrypted blob is safe to publish; only the passphrase (which exists nowhere but your head) unlocks it.

You will type this once on each new device. Random words are strong and typeable — use the dice button, or invent your own (avoid names, dates, lyrics, or anything about goblins or languages: crackers try meaningful phrases first).

Then commit and push. The landing page will show a passphrase field on devices that don't know the URL yet. PBKDF2-SHA256 × 1,500,000 iterations + AES-256-GCM.
